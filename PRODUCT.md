## Platform
web
## Users
College final-year project students, beginners and researchers, as explicitly requested by the user.
## Product Purpose
Explain and explore synthetic T+1 settlement risk and funding policy comparisons.
## Capabilities and Constraints
React local dashboard, FastAPI and a separate worker; synthetic data, calibrated risk models, empirical timing, saved policy comparisons. Preserve API flows, honest reference-policy probability explanations and research limitations.
## Brand Commitments
User requested blue accents, black dark theme, soft white light theme, consistent typography, custom dropdowns and a visible app icon. User now requests a replacement beginner-friendly dashboard.
## Product Principles
Explain terms near use. Make next actions clear. Preserve reproducibility. Distinguish predictions from realised results.
